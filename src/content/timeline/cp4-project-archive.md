The Lab's project archive was rebuilt around current engineering evidence.

Six substantial systems are now represented: VAJRA, AstraUserbot, VGU Signal, TelDrive Lab, GeminiAgentBridge and Astra.

The important decision was to curate rather than mirror. A project belongs here because it demonstrates meaningful engineering work, not simply because a repository exists.

Each project now explains its problem, constraints, architecture, verification approach and current engineering lesson.
