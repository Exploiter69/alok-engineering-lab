The Engineering Lab began as a small Astro project and evolved into a content-driven personal engineering archive.

The initial foundation established Projects, Writing, Notes, Experiments, Timeline, reusable content cards, detail pages, navigation, and a homepage dashboard.

The important decision was to build the Lab around structured content rather than treating it as a static portfolio.

This foundation became the base for everything that followed.
