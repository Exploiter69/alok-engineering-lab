A polished interface is not complete when it merely looks correct.

CP21 treats navigation state, keyboard traversal, accessible names, form labels, focus visibility, responsive target sizes and interaction behavior as testable engineering properties.

The important boundary is restraint: the Lab becomes more reliable without adding a large client-side framework or decorative complexity.
