The useful outcome of CP20 is not a new visual skin. It is a clearer mapping between the Lab's product model and its interface.

Projects now read as engineering records. Garden surfaces read as connected knowledge. Evidence reads as proof. Timeline reads as chronology. Writing gives the actual content more room to breathe.

The design stays intentionally restrained because the interface should help visitors understand the work rather than compete with it.
