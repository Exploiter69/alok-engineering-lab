CP11 makes the Lab's project-to-knowledge relationship bidirectional.

Project pages already exposed linked writing, notes and experiments. Knowledge detail pages now surface their related project records as explicit engineering provenance.

The implementation reuses the existing repository-backed related relationship model. No database, search service, runtime API, analytics or paid service was introduced.
