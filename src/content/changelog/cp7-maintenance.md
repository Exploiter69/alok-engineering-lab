## Added

- build-blocking validation for content relationships
- explicit maintenance and release discipline
- durable guidance for growing the knowledge archive
- continued zero-cost verification

## Direction

CP7 changes the Lab's focus from initial construction to sustainable maintenance.

The archive should grow through real engineering work rather than cosmetic iteration. Content relationships, builds and browser behavior remain part of the verification boundary.

## Constraint

No new paid service, database, CMS, analytics system or framework migration was introduced.
