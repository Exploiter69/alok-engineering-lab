## Question

Can the Lab grow its knowledge archive by extracting principles that are already demonstrated by its projects?

## Method

Compare project architecture, constraints, verification boundaries and design lessons. Record only patterns that recur across more than one system or are strong enough to stand as a reusable engineering rule.

## Result

The approach produced durable themes around authority boundaries, durable state, independent verification, bounded complexity and disposable derived views.

## Boundary

This is not a content-generation pipeline. The repository remains the source of truth, and a principle belongs in the knowledge archive only when it can be traced back to actual engineering work.

## Lesson

The archive should grow from engineering evidence outward, not from a quota for publishing inward.
