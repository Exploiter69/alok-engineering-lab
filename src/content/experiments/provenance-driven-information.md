## Question

Can a student-facing information system keep source trust visible while still making information easy to consume?

## Setup

Treat source, evidence, extraction, verification and delivery as separate stages.

A message delivered to a student should remain traceable to the evidence that produced it.

## Observation

Separating acquisition from delivery prevents a Telegram message from becoming the source of truth.

It also makes changed or superseded information easier to model instead of silently replacing history.

## Result

Provenance is useful as a product feature, not only an internal engineering concern.
